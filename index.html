Underbelly Navigation

The quickest way between rooms in the Underbelly beneath the Leviathan raid in Destiny 2. Add stops to plan a whole run, like going from one encounter to the next and grabbing a chest on the way.

Times are rough. The walk from Royal Pools through the Aqueduct, Transfer and Armory to the Gauntlet is timed from Phil Nelson's 2018 run, and every other link uses the average of those, seconds. Routes only go through an encounter when there's no other way. Rooms that aren't loot rooms or encounters aren't named on the community map by u/addesso, updated by u/ScoobyDeezy.
